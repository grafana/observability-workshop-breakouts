# Lab 3 - When everything breaks at once

This time the `ecommerce` store is throwing errors - 500s across multiple pages, coming and going. Several services look unhealthy at once, and it's not obvious which one is the cause and which are collateral damage.

In this lab you'll use the **[Knowledge Graph](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana-cloud/knowledge-graph/introduction/)**, which automatically models your applications and infrastructure into a graph of **entities** (services, pods, nodes, namespaces, databases) and their **relationships**, with continuously generated health **insights**. You'll work with its three surfaces:

- **Entity Catalog** - a searchable inventory of everything, with health at a glance
- **Entity Graph** - the visual dependency map
- **RCA Workbench** - a timeline of insights showing what failed, in what order

Because the failure is a crash-looping pod, you'll also use **[Kubernetes Monitoring](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana-cloud/monitor-infrastructure/kubernetes-monitoring/)** for the infrastructure detail - the OOMKill, the memory climbing into its limit, and right-sizing - plus **Drilldown** for the logs and traces, and the **Grafana Assistant** to close it out.

## Instructions

Use the Grafana Cloud stack from the slides; this lab uses the `ecommerce` app running in Kubernetes.

:::info When does this scenario run?

The memory-leak scenario runs automatically **every day during the `{LAB3_WINDOW}` window** (default 12:00-13:00 UTC - your facilitator will share today's exact times). Set your Grafana **time range** to cover that window, starting a little before it. The feature flag flips on at the start of the window - that flip is the "first event" you'll be looking for on the RCA timeline.

:::

You have around 20 minutes to work through the questions.

:::tip

Try each question on your own first. The **Show answer** block under each question reveals the walkthrough and screenshots.

:::
