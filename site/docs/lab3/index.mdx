# Lab 3 - Map the whole system with the Knowledge Graph

This time the `ecommerce` store is throwing errors - 500s across multiple pages, coming and going. Something is genuinely broken, several services look unhappy at once, and it's not obvious which one is the cause and which are just collateral damage.

When a problem has a wide blast radius, what you need first is a **map**: every service and piece of infrastructure, how they connect, what's unhealthy, and *what changed first*. That's the **[Knowledge Graph](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana-cloud/knowledge-graph/introduction/)**.

## What this lab teaches

The Knowledge Graph automatically models your applications and infrastructure into one connected graph of **entities** (services, pods, nodes, namespaces, databases...) and their **relationships**, and continuously attaches **insights** about their health. You'll use its three surfaces:

- **Entity Catalog** - a searchable inventory of everything, with health at a glance
- **Entity Graph** - the visual dependency map, for blast radius
- **RCA Workbench** - a timeline of insights that shows what failed, in what order

You'll find the unhealthy entity, see how far the damage spreads, trace the incident back to the **first event that caused it**, and drop into **Drilldown** for the logs and traces that confirm it - then let the **Grafana Assistant** narrate the whole thing.

:::info Why a graph?

Individual dashboards each show you one entity. A graph shows you the *relationships* - which is exactly what you need when the symptom (frontend errors) and the cause (a crashing backend service) are several hops apart.

:::
