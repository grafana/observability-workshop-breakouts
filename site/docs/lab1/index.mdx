# Lab 1 - The customer sees it first

Support tickets are trickling in about the `ecommerce` store: *"the site feels sluggish,"* *"product images take forever to appear."* You open your backend dashboards - CPU is calm, error rates are flat, every service is healthy. Nothing is on fire. And yet customers are clearly hurting.

This is the blind spot of backend-only monitoring. The experience lives in a place your server-side telemetry can't reach: the user's browser, on their device, on their network. **[Frontend Observability](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana-cloud/monitor-applications/frontend-observability/)** is how you see it.

## What this lab teaches

This workshop is a guided tour of Grafana Cloud's observability suite - one signal, one story at a time. This first stop is **Frontend Observability**, Grafana's Real User Monitoring (RUM) product, powered by the open-source [Faro Web SDK](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana-cloud/monitor-applications/frontend-observability/instrument/faro/). It streams telemetry straight from every visitor's browser: Core Web Vitals, page loads, errors with de-obfuscated stack traces, full user sessions, and browser traces that link into your backend.

You'll walk the app the way a real investigation flows - from a top-level health read, down to the exact resource that's slow, to a single user's journey, and finally into the backend trace to prove where the time *isn't* being spent. Along the way you'll learn what each view is for and the question it answers.

:::info Why start at the frontend?

It's the earliest, most honest signal you have. A degraded experience shows up here **before** it shows up as churn - and often when every backend metric still looks perfect.

:::

## Instructions

Go to the URL in the slides for your Grafana Cloud stack. This lab uses the `ecommerce` app.

:::info When does this scenario run?

The slow-images scenario fires automatically **every day during the `{LAB1_WINDOW}` window** (default 06:00-08:00 UTC - your facilitator will share today's exact times). Set your Grafana **time range** to cover that window; starting slightly before it lets you see the healthy baseline and the moment the regression begins.

:::

You have around 20 minutes. Work each question yourself first, then open **Show answer** for the walkthrough.

:::tip

Every answer names the exact view and steps to get there, so you can use this lab as a reference later.

:::
